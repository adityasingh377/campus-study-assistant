# Campus Study Assistant

> An AI-powered exam preparation assistant that helps college students decide what to study first when exam time is limited.

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://campus-study-assistant.lovable.app

---

## 📌 Overview

College students often have a large syllabus, multiple study resources, and very limited time before exams.

Campus Study Assistant helps students turn their **syllabus + previous-year questions (PYQs) + available preparation time + exam goal** into a prioritized study strategy.

Instead of simply generating more study material, the product focuses on answering:

> **"What should I study first?"**

This project was built as an MVP to explore how AI can support exam preparation through prioritization and time-aware planning.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Students preparing for exams often face four problems:

- Large and difficult-to-cover syllabi
- Study material scattered across different sources
- Difficulty identifying important or repeated topics from PYQs
- Limited preparation time, especially during the final days before an exam

Through user research, I found that students often use previous-year questions, notes, online resources, and AI tools to decide what to study.

The opportunity was not simply to provide another source of information, but to help students **prioritize their limited study time**.

---

## 🔎 User Research

I conducted interviews with **11 college students** from different colleges and academic disciplines to understand how they prepare for exams.

### Key findings

**1. PYQs are an important study signal**

Students commonly use previous-year questions to identify repeated or important topics.

**2. Students often prepare under time pressure**

Many students begin serious preparation only a few days before an examination.

**3. Study resources are fragmented**

Students use combinations of class notes, textbooks, SOL material, YouTube, PYQ books, senior notes, and AI tools.

**4. Different subjects require different preparation approaches**

Humanities may require arguments and critical readings, while science-related subjects may require practicals, calculations, diagrams, or procedures.

**5. Students already use AI**

Several students were already using ChatGPT for explanations, summaries, and PYQ analysis.

### Research insight

> Students don't necessarily need more content. They need help deciding what deserves their limited time first.

---

## 💡 Product Hypothesis

If students provide:

- Their syllabus
- Previous-year questions
- Available preparation time
- Exam goal

then an AI-assisted system can analyze the available information and generate a prioritized, time-aware study strategy.
